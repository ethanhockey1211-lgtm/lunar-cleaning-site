Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Lunar Cleaning ("the game") is made by Ethan Hagen ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your game progress (credits, upgrades, planets, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers, and we do not receive this data.
  • There are no accounts, logins or chat in the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional ads between planets. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and in-app ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Map › Privacy options in the game.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Buying No Ads or Lunar Tycoon turns off the ads between planets.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, so the game can deliver what you bought.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device. Map › Reset progress in the game, or uninstalling the game, deletes it. To ask about data held by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
